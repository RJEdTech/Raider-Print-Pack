Raider Print Pack
Print a whole assignment at once

Turn every student's work on a Canvas assignment or New Quiz into one PDF, one student per page, so you print once instead of opening papers one by one. Everything runs in your browser. Student work never leaves your computer.

STEP 1 Get the file from Canvas

Which kind of assignment are you printing? Pick one and follow the pictures.

  1. Open the quiz in Canvas
    Go to your course, then Quizzes or Assignments, and click the quiz. New Quizzes have a rocket icon. The quiz opens with a row of tabs across the top: Build, Settings, Moderate, Reports, Exports.
  2. Click Reports, then Generate Report under Student Analysis
    Click the Reports tab (1). In the Student Analysis box, click Generate Report (2). Wait a few seconds until the date under “Data Last Updated” changes to right now. That makes sure every student who has finished is included.
  3. Click Export CSV
    In the same Student Analysis box, click Export CSV (3). A file called student_analysis.csv lands in your Downloads folder. You don't need to open it. Come back to this page.
  1. Open the assignment in Canvas
    Go to your course, then Assignments, and click the assignment. Make your browser window full size. On a small window, Canvas hides the menu you need.
  2. Click Download Submissions on the right
    Look for Related Items on the right side of the page and click Download Submissions.
  3. Wait for the blue bar to fill
    Canvas gathers everyone's files into one .zip file. When the bar is full, it downloads on its own. If it doesn't, click Click here to download. Don't unzip it. Come back to this page.

STEP 2 Drop the file here

Drag the file from your Downloads folder onto the box, or click the box and choose it.

Drop student_analysis.csv or the .zip here
or click to choose it from Downloads

Within a few seconds every student appears in the list below, with how many pages each one will take. Check that nobody is missing. Print by hand means that one file needs to be printed on its own.

STEP 3 Print

Click Print. Your printer window opens with every student's work in it. Pick your printer and click Print.

No printer window? , then press Ctrl + P (on a Mac, ⌘ + P).

Good to know
  • New Quizzes: only essay question answers are printed. Multiple choice and other question types are left out. If a quiz has more than one essay, each answer is labeled with its question number (Question 2, Question 5…), in quiz order, one after another under that student's name.
  • Assignments: Word documents, PDFs, photos, and text typed into Canvas all print. Anything else (a Pages file, a Google link, a video) is listed as Print by hand so nobody gets missed.
  • Pictures that students pasted inside a New Quiz essay don't come through. The writing does.
  • Word documents are redrawn in your browser, so a font you don't have may look slightly different. The words and page order are the same.
